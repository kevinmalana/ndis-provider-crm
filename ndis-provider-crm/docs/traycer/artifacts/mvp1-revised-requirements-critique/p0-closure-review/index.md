## Remaining planning blockers (priority order)

### 1. Screening applicability for unregistered providers is a blocking policy gap — P0 / 05b planning blocker

**Locations.** Technical plan provider/evidence boundary and worker readiness sections (lines 26, 99–109); reference flow line 24; 05b lines 30–37; decision log 2026-08-10 screening decision (around lines 336–353).

**Breaks.** The readiness projection cannot decide whether an unregistered provider with no NDIS clearance is eligible, whether the provider has voluntarily made screening mandatory, or when a role is risk-assessed. Treating every provider as legally clearance-required contradicts current Commission guidance; treating every unregistered provider as warning-only contradicts the product’s stated hard-block policy and participant/provider choices.

**Correction.** Add an explicit provider policy and role classification to the scope/readiness contract. At minimum persist `screening_required` (provider policy), `risk_assessed` (role decision), jurisdiction, effective interval, and the reason/status used by the readiness projection. Then define the hard-block predicate for registered and voluntarily-screened unregistered providers separately from provider competence requirements.

### 2. Acknowledgement event state machine is still under-specified — P0 / 05b planning blocker

**Locations.** Technical plan lines 118–124; 05b lines 47–52; reference flow lines 24–25; existing free-text `representative_authorities.authority_type` at `supabase/migrations/0004_v1_domain_tables.sql:131–152`.

**Breaks.** An implementation can accept two signed events, an attempt after a signed event, or a correction with no deterministic current outcome. It can also accept a reported signer whose authority row is not the permitted type at the reported event time. The current schema has no authority-type check and no acknowledgement relation at all.

**Correction.** Specify the allowed event-type enum, event-time authority join, one current-view query/materialized projection, precedence/supersession rule, and concurrency/idempotency behavior. Require the actor’s active admin/scheduler membership for provider-recorded events; require the participant self-link or effective representative authority only for Ticket 08 direct events. Add tests for duplicate, competing, superseding, expired/revoked authority, and cross-tenant events.

## Non-blocking clarifications

- Keep “all providers may onboard” labelled as a product onboarding choice. It is not broad workflow coverage or a legal applicability engine. Specialist modules remain phased/not supported.
- Keep “provider-recorded screening/competence verification” distinct from live Commission verification and from a legal determination. The Commission places responsibility for worker competence/training on providers; metadata is not proof that a certificate or qualification was independently verified.
- Keep the exact elapsed Start/End snapshot distinct from billable quantity, pricing, and claims. The NDIA record-keeping page requires quantity/hours and support type for records; this ticket’s explicit time-only boundary is appropriate, but the product must not call it claim-ready.
- Keep office-recorded external signed/declined evidence distinct from participant-authenticated acknowledgement. The current NDIA support-log guidance identifies the participant, child representative, nominee, or legal guardian as signers; direct in-product confirmation belongs to Ticket 08.
- Urgent/incident/complaint destination configuration and handoff receipts (original P1.1) remain a prerequisite for Ticket 06’s worker route and pilot readiness, not for the 05b data foundation if 05b does not expose the route.
- Access/correction runbook, retention schedule, and privacy/security production review (original P1.4) remain before real-data pilot approval, not before synthetic 05b implementation.

## Final recommendation

**NOT READY for execution.** Under the corrected two-axis classification, P0.1 and P0.6 are contract-closed but runtime-open, and the missing admin UI is ordinary 05b scope. Ticket 05b still has two genuine pre-start blockers: registered-versus-unregistered screening applicability and acknowledgement precedence/authority transitions. Resolve those two product/architecture decisions, then Ticket 05b is **READY WITH NON-BLOCKING CLARIFICATIONS** to begin; its migration/UI/test work remains mandatory acceptance before Ticket 06.
